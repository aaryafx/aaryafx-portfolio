Info

I am Aarya Patel, a Senior Video Editor based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, with four years shooting and editing high-impact short-form and brand content. I specialise in fast-cut, trend-driven edits with motion graphics built into the cut rather than laid over it.

I run the chain end to end: concept, shoot, edit, colour grade and sound. One person across the whole process means fewer handoffs and a cut that keeps its intent. Working in English, Gujarati and Hindi.

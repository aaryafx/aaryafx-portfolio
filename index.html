About Work

Video editor cutting brand films, sport and social from first frame to final grade.

Senior Video Editor with four years shooting and cutting end to end, helping brands make work that actually holds attention.

Info

I am Aarya, a video editor and content creator based in Ahmedabad, India, with around four years across brand, retail and sport. I work as a full-stack shooter-editor: on set with the camera, then in the timeline until the story lands.

My strengths are colour grading and fast-cut motion graphics. One person across the whole chain means fewer handoffs and a cut that keeps its intent.

Who am I??

Hi, I am Aarya. I edit video out of Ahmedabad, India, and I have been doing it professionally for about four years.

I work as a full-stack shooter-editor. That means I am on set with the camera, and then I am in the timeline until the story actually lands. Not two people, one workflow.

What I care about most is pace. An edit has to earn the first three seconds, then keep you to the last frame. Everything else is in service of that.

Professional experience

  • Senior Video Editor, Craywingz. Leading edits across brand, retail and sport.
  • Shooter-editor on location. Campaign days, match days, event coverage.
  • Colour for brand films and long-form, grading in Resolve.
  • Motion graphics and fast-cut social edits built in After Effects.

Tools

  • Adobe Premiere Pro - the main timeline, long form and social.
  • After Effects - motion graphics, titles, clean-up.
  • DaVinci Resolve - colour grading and finishing.
  • On-set camera - shooting the footage I go on to cut.

What I shoot and cut

  • Brand films - the long one that sets the tone.
  • Reels and shorts - vertical, fast, built to be rewatched.
  • Sport - match day energy, quick turnarounds.
  • Retail and campaign - festive pushes and product launches.
